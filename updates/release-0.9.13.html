Prism Explorer · 0.9.13 (28)

Keep command details private.

Long-command notifications now show the result and elapsed time. Your command and folder path stay out of the default notification.

Choose when to include details

Turn on Include command and folder in notifications in Settings › Terminal when you want those details visible in Notification Center.

Removed drives stay out of the list

A slower refresh no longer brings back a drive you just removed, or restores its old name, in the sidebar and Go menu.

Notifications apply to commands lasting at least 10 seconds that finish while Prism is in the background, with notification permission.

Earlier repairs: terminal sizing, tab focus and folder columns
